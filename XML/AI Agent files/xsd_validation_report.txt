XSD VALIDATION: PASS

Museum.xml validates against Museum.xsd.
Themes.xml validates independently against Themes.xsd.
Collections.xml validates independently against Collections.xsd.

Artifacts.xml, Exhibitions.xml, and Restoration.xml contain xs:IDREF values whose xs:ID targets live in other section files.
They are therefore validated together in Museum.xml, where all cross-file ID and IDREF values share one XML document.
